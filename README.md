## 🚀 Automation Projects

### 🤖 HireFlow — Intelligent CV Screener & Interview Scheduler
> AI reads every CV, scores each candidate, gets human approval, then books the interview — fully automated

- Scans a Google Drive folder and processes every CV automatically
- Google Gemini 2.0 reads each PDF, extracts candidate details, evaluates experience and qualifications, and makes a hire/no-hire recommendation
- HR receives a structured summary email with a one-click Approve or Reject button
- On approval: an AI agent checks Google Calendar availability and books the interview slot automatically
- On rejection: the system moves to the next CV without any manual input
- **Tools:** n8n · Google Gemini 2.0 · Google Drive · Google Calendar · Gmail
- 🔗 [View Project](https://github.com/didi-devs/hireflow-cv-screener)
