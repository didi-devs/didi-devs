Happy Father's Day to our Daddy,
Daddy Prince of the Oladejo Dynasty! 🎉

There are men who exist in a child's life, and then there are fathers who live for their children — and you, Daddy, have always been the latter. The sleepless nights, the burdens you carry, the sacrifices you make quietly without complaint — we see it all, we feel it all, and we are better because of it.

And God Himself has been watching too. His Word declares in Hebrews 6:10 —

"For God is not unrighteous to forget your work and labour of love, which ye have showed toward his name, in that ye have ministered to the saints, and do minister."

Every sacrifice you made for this family Heaven has recorded. Not one has been forgotten.

Today we lift these prayers over your life:

"The LORD shall open unto thee his good treasure, the heaven to give the rain unto thy land in his season, and to bless all the work of thine hand: and thou shalt lend unto many nations, and thou shalt not borrow."
— Deuteronomy 28:12
"The LORD shall command the blessing upon thee in thy storehouses, and in all that thou settest thine hand unto."
— Deuteronomy 28:8

May God honour you the way you have honoured your responsibility to us.
